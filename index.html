Open question: Ice Bolt vs. Fire Mastery order

Which should you max first after Fire Ball, at level 35+?
Take Ice Bolt before Fire Mastery.
  • The surplus removes the tradeoff. With 9–14 spare points by clvl 99, this isn't "can I afford both" — it's purely about which lands sooner. You max both either way.
  • Ice Bolt buffs your primary skill. It's Frozen Orb's synergy, and Frozen Orb is mapped to your opener (X). Fire Mastery only helps Fire Ball, which the note already frames as the cold-immune / single-target backup on Y.
  • Earlier Ice Bolt points compound sooner. Every point invested while leveling from 35→70ish is buffing the skill you're actually opening every pack with, versus a flat multiplier sitting on your secondary skill until you get around to it.
  • Nothing is lost by going Ice Bolt first. Since both hit 20/20 by end-game under either order, there's no final-build cost — only a smoother mid-level clear speed curve.
